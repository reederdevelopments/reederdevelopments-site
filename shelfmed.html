Engineered for Household Safety

Inside the ShelfMed Architecture

Every feature in ShelfMed is purpose-built to make managing family health effortless, private, and medically organized.

AI OCR Box & Barcode Scanner

Point your camera at any medicine box or pharmacy label. On-device Google ML Kit text recognition and barcode scanning auto-detect the medicine name, schedule (S0–S6), quantity, and expiry date.

190+ SA Medicines Catalog

Includes an embedded offline catalog of over 190 common South African medications plus OpenFDA & WHO INN clinical lookups for active ingredients, schedules, and standard uses.

Zero-Cloud QR Family Sync

Your medical records stay in local SQLite storage. Scan a family QR code to pair household phones and sync doses & inventory peer-to-peer with AES-256 encryption, plus optional encrypted Google Drive backup.

FEFO Smart Dose Logging

Log single or multiple medications at once for any family member. ShelfMed automatically selects the earliest-expiring bottle first (FEFO) and deducts remaining tablets or milliliters.

Interaction Checker & SOS Hub

Built-in medication interaction alerts alongside a rapid-dial Emergency & Poison Control directory (South Africa, US, UK, Global 112/911) and custom family emergency contacts.

Clinical Reports & Charts

Generate visual usage charts by family member, inspect expired or expiring-soon items for safe disposal, check low-stock refills, and view a clean Doctor-Ready Cabinet Summary.
