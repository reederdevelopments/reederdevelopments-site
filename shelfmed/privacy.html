Privacy Policy for ShelfMed

Published by ReederDevelopments • Package: com.reederdevelopments.shelfmed

1 Introduction

ReederDevelopments respects your privacy. We built ShelfMed with a privacy-first, local-first architecture. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled, collected, and protected when using the ShelfMed mobile application.

2 Data Storage & Medical Privacy (Zero Cloud Policy)

ShelfMed operates without central cloud servers or remote databases storing your personal health information.

  • Local Storage: All personal details, family profiles, medicine cabinet inventories, and consumption logs remain stored locally on your device using an encrypted local database.
  • No Server Access: We cannot view, access, store, or sell your medical cabinet data or history.

3 Device-to-Device Synchronization & Encryption

ShelfMed supports household synchronization between devices without uploading unencrypted data to external servers.

  • Local Network Sync: Devices connected to the same local Wi-Fi network communicate directly with each other using peer-to-peer protocols (mDNS/Bonjour).
  • Remote Relay: When devices are on separate networks (e.g., cellular data), data payloads pass through a WebSockets routing relay to locate your family device.
  • End-to-End Encryption (E2EE): All sync payloads are encrypted on-device using an AES-256 key before transmission. The relay server cannot read or decrypt your synchronization data.

4 Third-Party Services

ShelfMed integrates with specific third-party SDKs and APIs to provide application functionality:

  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage metrics to serve ads. View Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Google ML Kit: Performs on-device text recognition for scanning medication labels. ML Kit processes image frame data entirely on your local device without sending camera images to external servers.
  • OpenFDA API: Used to query public clinical medicine details for auto-populating medication information. Search queries sent to OpenFDA include standard network request metadata (such as IP address) required for HTTP transmission.

5 Permissions Requested

To deliver its features, ShelfMed requests the following device permissions:

Permission Purpose
Camera Scans medicine bottle labels and QR codes for family sync pairing.
Notifications & Alarms Delivers scheduled medication reminders on time.
Network & Wi-Fi State Discovers family devices on local Wi-Fi networks and routes encrypted sync messages.

6 Data Control & Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your data. Because all database records reside on your physical device, you can permanently wipe all stored app data at any time by:

  1. Clearing app data via your Android System Settings, or
  2. Uninstalling the ShelfMed application from your device.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published directly to this page with an updated effective date.
