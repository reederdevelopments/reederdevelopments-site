Built for Market Rushes & Pop-Up Traders

Everything Your Stall Needs in One Offline POS

No monthly POS subscriptions, no card-machine lock-in, and zero internet required. Engineered for speed when a line of 20 customers is waiting at your stall.

1. Stall Command Center & 1-Tap Rush Grid

See Today’s Sales Value, All-Time Revenue, Average Basket Size, and a live Cash vs. Card progress bar at a glance. Tap + on your Most Popular Items directly on the home dashboard to load the checkout cart in milliseconds during a customer rush.

2. 3-Tier Automated Discount & Combo Engine

Configure Day-of-Week Specials (e.g., 15% off on Saturdays), Multi-Buy Quantity Deals (Buy 2 Get 1 Free, 2 for R50 bundle pricing, Buy X+ Get % or R Off), and Connected-Item Combos (e.g., Burger + Cold Drink = 15% Off) that trigger automatically in the cart.

3. Instant WhatsApp & Email Customer Receipts

No thermal printer required! Complete a Cash or Card sale, calculate exact customer change due, and send an itemized digital receipt showing line items, combo savings, optional VAT/Sales Tax, and daily invoice number (yyyyMMdd_0001) via WhatsApp or Email.

4. Multi-Level Folders, 4 Views & Made-to-Order (∞)

Organize products into Category 1 & Category 2 folders with cropped photos. Switch between 2x, 3x, 4x Grids or List view, toggle Green Edit Mode to restock or edit items, and mark fresh items like coffee or food as Made-to-Order (`∞`) or tracked stock with low-stock push alerts.

5. Itemized Partial Refunds, Charts & CSV Export

Need to refund a single item or an entire sale? Select specific line-item quantities, choose whether the refund was paid in Cash or Card, and automatically return refunded items to inventory stock. View separate Refunds Breakdowns and export Sales & Refunds CSV spreadsheets.

6. Multi-Stall Profiles, Sunlight Mode & AES-256 Vault

Run separate stalls on one device—each with its own currency (11 currencies including R ZAR, $ USD, € EUR, £ GBP), PIN/Password/Biometric lock, Outdoor Sunlight High-Contrast Mode, and passphrase-protected AES-256 `.mystall` backups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Market Stall & Small Business POS FAQs

Does MyStall POS require Wi-Fi, mobile data, or a monthly subscription?

No. MyStall: POS & Inventory works 100% offline with zero monthly fees and no forced cloud accounts. All products, categories, photos, and sales invoices stay on your Android device.

What happens if I clear the checkout cart before completing a sale?

Clearing the checkout cart never increments or wastes your daily invoice sequence (yyyyMMdd_0001). Invoice numbers only increment when a sale is completed.

Can I sell items like coffee or cooked food that don’t have a fixed stock count?

Yes! When adding or editing a product, simply tick "No Quantity Required (Made-to-Order / ∞)". MyStall tracks total units sold and revenue for that item without deducting from a finite stock count.
