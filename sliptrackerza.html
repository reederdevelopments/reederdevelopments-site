South African Shopper Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions About Slip Tracker ZA

How do I snap a slip and track grocery prices in South Africa for free?

Download Slip Tracker ZA (v1.2.0) free on Google Play and tap Scan Slip. Snap a photo of any South African till slip—its on-device AI OCR reads the store, date, line items, discounts, and 15% VAT offline and automatically compares item prices across 150+ SA stores.

How can I split a restaurant bill and waiter tip on WhatsApp in Rands?

Open the Bill Split tab in Slip Tracker ZA and photograph your restaurant bill. Multi-quantity items (2x, 3x) are split into single rows automatically so each person can tap their own items, split shared starters, add waiter tip, share the breakdown via WhatsApp, and save only your share!

How do I check if the till price matches the shelf price while shopping?

Use the Shopping Cart & Shelf Tag Calculator while walking the aisles to snap shelf price tags. You’ll see your live running trolley total before reaching the till, and after paying you can run the Cart vs. Till Slip Comparison to spot any overcharged items.

Can I track company reimbursements, petrol slips, and SARS tax claims?

Yes. Tag slips with #Business or #Fuel, record fuel litres and odometer readings, mark employer-reimbursed items (and optionally credit the reimbursed Rands back to your monthly budget), and export filtered SARS / Business Claim CSV spreadsheets.
