Engineered for South African Shoppers

Inside the Slip Tracker ZA Engine

Purpose-built to handle real South African retail slip formats while keeping 100% of your financial data private on your phone.

Custom SA Receipt OCR Parser

Trained on real South African till slips. Automatically parses single and multi-line QTY @ R PRICE items, XtraSave and Less cash-off loyalty discounts, zero-rated (#V / *) tax flags, and 15% VAT breakdowns.

16+ Preloaded SA Retailers

Ships pre-configured with Checkers, Shoprite, Pick n Pay, Woolworths, SPAR, Clicks, Dis-Chem, Makro, Game, Builders Warehouse, Boxer, Food Lover's Market, PEP, Crazy Plastics & more—plus custom store creation.

Cross-Store Price Comparison

Uses intelligent string similarity matching to group identical or similar products across different retailers so you can immediately see which store sells your regular items at the lowest unit price.

Monthly Budgets & Visual Reports

Set default or month-specific spending targets, filter analytics by date range or store, and inspect visual charts showing your top-purchased items, store spending shares, and total loyalty savings.

100% Local & AES-256 Backups

No cloud accounts or bank logins required. All slips and cropped receipt images stay on your device, with one-tap export to password-encrypted .sliptracker backup files (including embedded Base64 receipt photos).

Full Line-Item Control & Themes

Review and edit any scanned line item, quantity, discount, or VAT amount before saving. Includes Light Mode, Dark Slate Mode, and an accessible High-Contrast Mode.
